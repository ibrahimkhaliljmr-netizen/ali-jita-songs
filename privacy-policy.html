Privacy Policy for Ali Jita Songs

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

Welcome to Ali Jita Songs ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location directly.

  • Local Storage: Custom playlists and app preferences are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database and DataStore preferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Audio Streaming: Music tracks are streamed over secure HTTPS connections. Standard server request logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) may be processed by our audio hosting providers strictly for bandwidth management and playback buffering.

2. Google AdMob & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app to support our free service. Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our app or other apps on the internet.

  • You can manage your ad personalization settings through your Google Account settings under Google > Ads.
  • For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: Google Advertising Policy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

4. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you have specific data protection rights regarding your device data and advertising preferences. You may reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings.

5. Links to External Applications

The "More Apps" section contains direct links to official Google Play Store listings for related apps. Clicking these links will open the Google Play Store or external web browser. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer contact email listed on our Google Play Store listing.
